# Code of Conduct

This project exists for educational, defensive, and authorized security research. Contributors are expected to keep discussion professional, lawful, and focused on improving the playbook.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but be precise when correcting technical issues.
- Keep examples inside legal lab or authorized assessment contexts.
- Credit sources and prior research.
- Accept maintainer decisions about safety, scope, and quality.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Requests or instructions for unauthorized access.
- Sharing secrets, credentials, leaked data, or private infrastructure details.
- Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, or abusive language.
- Submitting destructive or intentionally harmful material.
- Pressuring maintainers to publish content that increases misuse risk.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, close, or remove issues and pull requests that do not match the project scope or conduct expectations. Repeated or severe violations may lead to being blocked from participating.
